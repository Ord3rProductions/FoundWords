Our mission

Our mission

When a stroke, Parkinson’s, ALS, or aphasia takes away someone’s speech, it doesn’t take away what they have to say. Found Words gives them a way to say it again — by tapping pictures of the people and things that matter most to them.

It began as one family’s answer to watching a loved one lose their words. It worked. So we made it free for every family who needs it — with no ads, no subscriptions, and no data ever leaving the device.

Who it’s for

Made for real lives, on both sides of the screen.

💬

People finding their voice

Adults with Parkinson’s, ALS, or MS; stroke and brain-injury survivors with aphasia; and anyone experiencing progressive speech loss.

🤝

The people beside them

Family members, partners, and adult children who set it up — plus speech-language pathologists and care staff who use it every day.

📷

Their actual world

A photo of their coffee mug says more than any generic icon. Found Words is built from real pictures of a real life.

How it works

Set up in minutes. Simple enough to use every day.

1

Add real photos

A caregiver snaps or uploads pictures of favorite foods, people, and places, and gives each one a label.

2

Organize into categories

Group cards by topic — Food & Drink, People, Activities, Needs — with clear colors and icons.

3

Tap to communicate

A full-screen, distraction-free board. Big cards, clear feedback, and it works offline once loaded.

Privacy first

Their photos never leave their device.

Found Words was designed so families never have to trade privacy for help. Everything lives locally in the browser — there is no server to leak, no account to breach, and nothing to sell.

  • ✓All photos and data stay on the device, in local storage.
  • ✓No accounts, no logins, nothing to sign up for.
  • ✓No analytics, no tracking, no cloud sync — ever.
  • ✓Backups are a plain file you own and control.
